My story

A year ago a few blood clots in my lung put me in the hospital. Months of tests and specialists later, I had a name for it: antiphospholipid syndrome. My blood clots when it shouldn't.

I walked out with a long list of medications and a warning that I couldn't work the way I used to. I was lost. I had built a lot of things, and suddenly none of it meant anything. I was afraid of not being there for my kids.

So I went to my friend and doctor, Dr. Ali K. He didn't start with my blood. He started with metabolism, and he showed me that metabolic health and mental health are one subject.

I changed three things: how I eat, how I think and how much pressure I put on myself, and how much I walk. It changed how I feel. That's my experience, not advice.

Now I'm sharing what I learn, one question at a time.
